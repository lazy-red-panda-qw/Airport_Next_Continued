## Introduction

Airport Details Pack Continued is the sequel to my earlier [Airport Details Pack](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/113207/Windows), developed by the same creator for Cities: Skylines II.

It provides modular ground markings and pavement surfaces for building detailed airports. Place individual decals, draw lines with NetLanes, paint areas with Surfaces, then combine them into runway, taxiway and apron layouts.

Version 0.7.0.0 includes **506 assets: 460 Decals, 39 NetLanes and 7 Surfaces**:

- Runway designators, threshold stripes, centre lines, aiming points, touchdown-zone blocks, side stripes, displaced-threshold bars and arrows, yellow chevrons and closure markings.
- Taxiway centre lines, enhanced centre lines, runway and ILS holding markings, intermediate holding lines and non-load-bearing pavement boundaries.
- Transparent white, black and yellow characters in 1 m, 2 m and 4 m sizes, decimal points, arrows, modular backgrounds and thin outlines.
- Ten apron bases, with independently placed stop markings, aircraft-type labels, bridge movement and wheel-position markings, no-parking areas and equipment parking outlines.
- Concrete and asphalt pavement with fine material detail, four paint colours and a transparent red no-parking hatch Surface.

Required dependencies are [Extra Assets Importer](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/80529/Windows), [Extra Detailing Tools](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/80528/Windows), and their required ExtraLib dependency. The intended game series is 1.6.*, including the author's current 1.6.2f setup. Ground markings are scenery components; players choose their positions, dimensions and combinations.

## Recommended companion assets

**G87 materials and markings** - I regularly use and recommend [Vanilla Asphalt](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/97145/Windows), [Vanilla Pavement](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/99944/Windows), [Road Markings](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/97828/Windows), and [Stripes and Chevrons](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/98624/Windows) for more pavement, ordinary road-marking and hatching choices.

**Airport props and lighting** - [Airport Pack by peijo](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/147286/Any) adds sign bodies and airfield props; [LAX Airport Props - No Landmark Buildings DLC](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/128482/Any) adds airport buildings, bridges and equipment; [Runway Guard Light by kjmci](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/150288/Any) adds a flashing holding-position light. Check each pack's own dependencies. Airport Pack sign inscriptions use Write Everywhere.

**Sully's Airport Essentials Pack** - I recommend [Airport Essentials Pack by Sully_Skylines](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/117064/Windows) for its high-quality tiled airport Surfaces, runway-edge options and complementary airport detailing assets.

These companion packs are **optional, not required dependencies**. They have not all been fully tested together with this edition. Select only what your airport needs, add a moderate number of packs, and check their game versions and dependencies before use.

## Current status

**0.7.0.0 is the first public WIP release.** Development and maintenance will continue, with future updates expanding service-vehicle road markings, purpose-specific apron markings and other airport details.

The main goal is to provide useful, modular airport markings with clear purposes and documented dimensions, using ICAO Annex 14 as the design baseline and identifying regional CAAC, CAAM and airport-specific variants separately. Package presets remain distinct from regulatory requirements. This is a scenery asset pack, not an ICAO certification or an airport-operation simulation.
